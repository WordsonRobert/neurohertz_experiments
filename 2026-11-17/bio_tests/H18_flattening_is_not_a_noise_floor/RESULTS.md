## Consequences
- **The floor is the same in both groups,** and it makes up the same share of 32–40 Hz power. The **neural** component alone is flatter and lower in AD. **R3 (a constant floor) does not explain the flattening.**
- **The flattening is not AD-specific.** FTD shows it to the same extent. So it is a marker of neurodegeneration in posterior cortex, not of AD alone. Specificity has to come from elsewhere (topography, beta loss, tau).
- **Not tested here:** a floor that varies between subjects with vigilance. Østergaard et al. 2024 report that vigilance shifts the exponent. The H9 vigilance strata are the existing control.

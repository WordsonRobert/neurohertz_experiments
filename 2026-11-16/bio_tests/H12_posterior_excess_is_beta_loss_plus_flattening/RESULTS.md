## Consequences
- **No absolute gamma increase.** Absolute 32–40 Hz power is not higher in AD.
- **What is lost is below 30 Hz.** AD loses power at 16–30 Hz, both the beta rhythm and the background under it, while 32–47 Hz holds. The "excess" is relative.
- **The robust finding is the flatter 24–47 Hz slope.** It replicates in every pipeline.
- **The bump is pipeline-dependent.** It needs muscle-cleaned raw data.
- **Target for the mechanism work (H13–H17):**
    1. posterior beta loss;
    2. high-frequency flattening;
    3. 32–40 Hz preserved in absolute terms.

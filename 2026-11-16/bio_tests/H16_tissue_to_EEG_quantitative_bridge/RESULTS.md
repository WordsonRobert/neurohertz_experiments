# Results · H16

## Main results
- **Tissue:** L2/3 IT loss 0.27 [0.07, 0.43]; total excitatory-drive loss 0.12 [0.03, 0.21].
- **Global variant:**

| Feature | Predicted | Observed |
|---|---|---|
| beta 16–23 Hz | −0.19 [−0.23, −0.14] | −0.35 ± 0.09 |
| 24–30 Hz | −0.15 | −0.18 ± 0.08 |
| 32–40 Hz | −0.11 | −0.06 ± 0.07 |
| slope | +0.24 [+0.04, +0.64] | +0.68 ± 0.23 |
| bump | +0.009 | +0.035 ± 0.013 |

    - All three phenotype signs (beta ↓, slope flatter, bump ↑) are right in **99%** of draws.
    - χ² 12.3 [6.1, 22.3], against 24.5 for the offset alone.
- **Superficial variant:** overshoots the slope (+1.5), and the bump sign is right in only 11% of draws. Rejected.

## Consequences
- **Direction:** the tissue-measured loss of superficial excitatory neurons, entering as a general loss of excitatory output, predicts the **direction** of every phenotype feature with nothing fitted to the EEG.
- **Size:** it predicts **about half** of the effect. This is expected as a lower bound, because tissue shares understate absolute neuron loss.
- **Not beta-loop-specific:** the loss is a general reduction of excitatory output, not one confined to the beta loop.

## Consequences
- **The fitting-artefact explanation is not supported.** Removing the background locally, without any curve over 2–25 Hz, leaves a posterior 32–40 Hz excess in AD of g ≈ 0.65. It is larger than with the global fit, and it remains after adjusting for slowing (p 0.011–0.033).
- **The excess is relative, not absolute.** Raw 32–40 Hz power does not differ (g −0.23). The finding is a bump at 32–40 Hz relative to the neighbouring 24–30 and 42–47 Hz, not more power.
- **Muscle is not excluded as a contributor.** In the lowest-EMG quarter of epochs, the local measures shrink (g 0.28–0.41) and are not significant at age + sex. This needs the burst-level separation from EMG (next step).
- **The posterior gamma line of work continues** to the burst and state analyses, as a candidate phenotype to freeze and test in another cohort.

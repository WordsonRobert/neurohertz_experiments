# Results · H13

## Main results
- **Calibration:**
    - RMS error on the CN spectrum: 0.017–0.018 log10;
    - beta resonance near 17 Hz;
    - no visible gamma resonance in the CN mean, consistent with 3% of CN having a peak (H6).
- **Observed target:**

| Feature | AD − CN | z |
|---|---|---|
| beta 16–23 Hz | −0.35 log10 | −3.8 |
| 24–30 Hz | −0.18 | −2.3 |
| 32–40 Hz | −0.06 | n.s. |
| 42–47 Hz | −0.04 | n.s. |
| slope | +0.68 | 2.9 |
| bump | +0.035 | 2.7 |

- **Fits** (χ², 6 features; no change 36.9; offset only 24.5):

| Mechanism | χ² (two calibration families) | Direction needed |
|---|---|---|
| M4 slow-loop inhibition ↓ | **0.6 / 3.4** | k ≈ 0.33–0.41 |
| M1 excitatory gain | **8.2 / 2.5** | **reversed: gain ↓** |
| M5 input E/I | 7.4 / ~24 | reversed |
| M2 PV→E inhibition ↓ | 23 / 15 | fails |
| M3 recurrent E ↑ | 24 / 18 | fails |
| best pair | ≤ 0.6 | no better than M4 alone |

## Consequences
- **Every "more excitation" mechanism moves the spectrum the wrong way:** beta up, slope steeper. They are rejected for this phenotype.
- **Two explanations remain:**
    - weaker slow (beta-generating) inhibition;
    - lower excitatory drive.
- **The bump comes for free.** It follows from losing the beta shoulder (the 24–30 Hz flank drops); gamma is untouched.
- **Rest alone can't separate the two survivors.** The M4 and reversed-M1 directions are nearly collinear (cosine −0.93 for the unsigned knobs). This is resolved by tissue (H15) and probes (H17).
- **Limits:**
    - single node, no spatial structure, no alpha generator;
    - knob magnitudes are modelling choices, so only directions and relative fit are interpreted;
    - several time constants sit at their bounds in some calibrations.

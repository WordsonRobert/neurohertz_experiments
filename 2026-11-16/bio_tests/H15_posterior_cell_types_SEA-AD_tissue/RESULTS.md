# Results · H15

## Main results
- **Positive control:** on all 83 MTG donors the method recovers the published early **SST** decline (ρ = −0.23, p = 0.04) and the L2/3 IT decline (ρ = −0.42, p < 0.001).
- **Same 43 donors**, ρ with local CPS:

| Region | L2/3 IT excitatory | Sst | Pvalb |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AnG (≈ P3/P4)** | **−0.46 (p = 0.002)**; high/low share 0.73 | −0.11 | +0.08 |
| V1C (≈ O1/O2) | −0.25 (ADNC p = 0.04) | +0.19 | +0.05 |
| MTG | −0.38 (p = 0.01) | −0.22 | −0.05 |
| DFC | −0.29 | +0.05 | +0.04 |
| ITG | −0.27 | −0.18 | +0.22 |
| STG | −0.22 | **−0.32 (p = 0.04)** | +0.26 |

- **GABAergic share of all neurons:** no change in any region.
- **Amyloid vs tau** (partial ρ for L2/3 IT):

| Region | pTau, controlling Aβ | Aβ, controlling pTau |
|---|---|---|
| AnG | **−0.37** | −0.01 |
| MTG | −0.46 | +0.15 |
| DFC | −0.35 | +0.09 |

- V1C is weak and goes the other way. The Aβ and pTau scores correlate at 0.58–0.72.

## Consequences
- **Under the posterior electrodes, superficial excitatory neurons are the ones lost.**
    - The L2/3 IT decline is largest in AnG, the region under P3, which matches the EEG topography.
    - SST and PV shares are unchanged there.
    - SST loss is temporal (STG, MTG).
- **Tissue picks the survivor.** It favours **lower excitatory drive** over slow-inhibition loss as the source of the posterior EEG phenotype.
- **The loss is tau-linked, not amyloid-linked,** in AnG, MTG and DFC.
- **Limits:**
    - these are shares within each class, not absolute counts;
    - n = 43 cannot exclude a mild SST decline in AnG;
    - different people from the EEG cohort.

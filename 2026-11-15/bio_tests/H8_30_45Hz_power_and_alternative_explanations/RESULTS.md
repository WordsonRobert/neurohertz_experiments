## Main results (AD vs CN)

**B. 30–45 Hz measures under covariate sets** (Freedman–Lane permutation p):

| Measure | Hedges g | p (age+sex) | p (+exponent) | p (+EMG) | p (+theta/alpha) | p (+all three) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw 30–45 Hz log power | −0.26 | 0.56 | 0.28 | 0.71 | 0.66 | 0.38 |
| relative 30–45 Hz power | 0.21 | 0.21 | 0.012 | 0.16 | 0.32 | 0.029 |
| power above 1/f fit (3–30 Hz) | 1.10 | <0.001 | 0.037 | <0.001 | 0.053 | 0.11 |
| power above floor fit | 0.76 | 0.017 | 0.042 | 0.017 | 0.17 | 0.12 |
| EMG (55–90 Hz, sentinel) | −0.29 | 0.60 | 0.70 | 0.59 | 0.48 | 0.89 |
| 1/f exponent | 1.11 | <0.001 | — | <0.001 | 0.11 | 0.11 |

**C. Region pattern of "power above floor":** g = 0.47 frontal, 0.34 central, 0.63 temporal, 0.75 parietal, 0.71 occipital. EMG g = −0.20 to 0.02 across regions.

**D. Within-group coupling with EMG:** raw 30–45 Hz power ρ = 0.68 (CN), 0.74 (AD), 0.79 (FTD). Power above floor ρ = 0.36, −0.03, 0.03.

**E. Harmonic test, presence, severity:**
- 49 subjects had a fitted 30–45 Hz peak.
- ρ(gamma peak, alpha peak) = −0.14; 33% were within 0.15 of an integer ratio (about 30% expected by chance).
- Oscillation presence: AD 14%, CN 4%, FTD 10%.
- MMSE within AD: all |ρ| ≤ 0.21.

## Consequences
- Raw 30–45 Hz power is not higher in AD.
- Measures of gamma "above the 1/f background" are higher in AD. The difference is reduced to non-significance by adjusting for slowing (theta/alpha), and it tracks the steeper 1/f exponent in AD.
- Muscle activity does not differ between groups. It dominates individual differences in raw 30–45 Hz power.
- No sign of alpha harmonics was found.
- Within these features, the AD signal at 30–45 Hz is attributable to the aperiodic slope and slowing. The frequency-resolved test in H9 checks this at 0.5 Hz × electrode resolution.

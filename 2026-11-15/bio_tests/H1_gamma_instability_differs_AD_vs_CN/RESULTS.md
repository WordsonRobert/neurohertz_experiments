## Consequences
- H1 is not supported in ds004504. No AD–CN difference in gamma burst-frequency variability was detected; the CI excludes differences larger than about 0.28 Hz in the AD > CN direction.
- Under the plan's gates, H2–H4 are reported as descriptive, not confirmatory.
- The positive control (P05) showed that `burst_freq_sd` can miss an injected instability at this sample size. The result therefore bounds the effect size; it does not show the absence of any instability.

# Results · P08

## Main results
- **Between-session ICC, ds004504 vs ds006036 (n = 54):**
    - `burst_freq_sd` −0.05;
    - aperiodic exponent 0.19; aperiodic offset 0.19;
    - gamma envelope mean −0.03; gamma IF median 0.04;
    - highest: theta relative power 0.50.
- **Participant tables:** identical for all 88 subjects on age, sex, group and MMSE.
- **Within-session split-half Spearman correlations:**
    - aperiodic exponent 0.97 (ds004504) and 0.94 (ds006036);
    - gamma envelope 0.97 and 0.95.
- **Cross-session Spearman correlations:** aperiodic exponent 0.17; envelope 0.00; alpha peak frequency −0.13.
- **Fingerprint match:** 7.4% of people matched their own profile (chance 1.9%). A one-to-one assignment matched 0 of 54.

## Consequences
- Measures that repeat within each session do not repeat across the two sessions, even though the participant tables identify the same people.
- The between-session ICCs therefore cannot be used as test–retest reliability.
- The source of the mismatch lies in the recordings or session conditions, not in the ID tables. ds006036 is described by its curators as an eyes-open photic session; the eyes-closed stretches used here are short.
- ds006036 can still support within-session AD vs CN comparisons (for example photic driving). It cannot serve as a retest set.
